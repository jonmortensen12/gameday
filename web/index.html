Or rehearse with a finished game

A rehearsal plays the real feed back from kickoff, as if live. Find the game's replay video, start it, and tap KICK on a phone at the opening kickoff - the game lines itself up with the video. P pauses along with the video.

How it works

Nobody has to guess anything until someone asks. On any phone: 📣 Call the next play gives everyone 30 s to call run or pass, and 🏈 New drive gives everyone 2 minutes to guess how the next drive goes. Whoever asked can lock everyone's guesses at the snap. If nobody starts a new drive, ESPN does when it reports the ball changing hands.
